# Life Wheel: A Random Life Simulation Game

## Project Proposal

This application is a **2D desktop life-simulation game** based on a spinning-wheel mechanic. The player takes the role of a person who is about to be born on Earth. At the beginning of the game, a wheel randomly assigns the player a country of birth. The assigned country may affect the player's starting conditions, available opportunities, and future life events.

Each time the player spins the wheel, the character becomes one year older. The result of each spin represents an event that occurs during that year of the character's life. These events may include education, health, family, friendships, employment, finances, relationships, or unexpected accidents.

The outcome of one event may affect the possible results of future spins. For example, receiving a good education may increase the probability of finding a high-paying job later, while experiencing a serious illness may reduce the character's health and limit future opportunities. The player's previous experiences, attributes, and choices will therefore influence how the character's life develops.

Throughout the game, the player will be able to view the character's age, country, health, wealth, education, career, relationships, and major life events. The game continues until the character reaches the end of their life. At that point, the player can review a summary of the character's complete life journey.

This game is intended for players who enjoy simulation games, random events, and games in which earlier outcomes influence later possibilities. I am interested in this project because I enjoy games with unpredictable results and branching life paths. I would like to create a game in which every playthrough produces a different life story.

## User Stories

- As a player, I want to be randomly assigned a country of birth so that each new game begins with different starting conditions.
- As a player, I want to spin a wheel to generate a random life event.
- As a player, I want my character's age to increase by one year after each spin.
- As a player, I want to view my character's current age, country, health, wealth, education, and other life attributes.
- As a player, I want previous life events to affect the possible outcomes of future spins.
- As a player, I want to add completed life events to my character's life history.
- As a player, I want to view all major events that have occurred during my character's life.
- As a player, I want to make choices during certain events that may affect my character's future.
- As a player, I want to save the current state of my character's life.
- As a player, I want to load a previously saved game and continue from where I stopped.
- As a player, I want to receive a summary of my character's life when the game ends.
